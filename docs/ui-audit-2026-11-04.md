# Holmes UI audit

Date: 2026-11-04 · Status: **audit only, nothing changed.** Code: `ui/warm-theme` at `a529b28`.

Every recruiter and applicant screen was reviewed: each tab, section, button, link, badge, field, column and visible text. Six questions were asked of each: is it needed, is it a duplicate, does it belong here, does it give the user value, can the user do without it, is it complex. Each item got one decision: KEEP, REMOVE, MERGE, MOVE, SIMPLIFY or RENAME.

The full item-by-item tables, with file and line references, are in `docs/ui-audit/`:
- `A-applicant.md`: sign-in, dashboard, the 19 form sections, review, submitted view
- `B-applications.md`: Applications list and search, application detail, status controls, modals
- `C-records-insights.md`: sidebar, Requirements, Candidates, detail pages, Dashboard, KPI
- `D-schedule-admin.md`: Calendar, Expiry, Settings, recruiter sign-in and setup, routes

The findings were made by reading the code, not by clicking through the app. Items marked *verified* below were rechecked against the code. Visual items should be confirmed in a browser before they are changed.

## Overall verdict

The app covers the job. Its problems fall into four groups:
1. **The same thing in several places.**
   - The same number appears up to four times: Requirements, Dashboard, KPI, and the stat cards on detail pages.
   - Application status can be changed from five or more places.
   - The KPI page has five export paths.
   - The application sections are listed four times on the applicant side, under five sets of names.
2. **Text that explains the obvious, or says something untrue.**
   - Untrue examples: "This action cannot be undone" (Reopen exists), "The candidate is notified" (no email is sent), "Shows up in Find Past Candidates" (no such feature).
   - About 60 helper lines in the long form just repeat their field label.
3. **A handful of real bugs**, some serious (section 1).
4. **Old-flow parts that case v1 will replace.** Test, interview, offer, documents and hired are still recorded on the application. These should not be polished; they move to the case (section 4).

Screens that are already lean: the sidebar, recruiter sign-in, password reset, the applicant sign-in, the applicant form sidebar, and the submitted-application view.

## 3. Rules to apply everywhere

1. **One word per thing:** "candidate", never "prospect" or "junction", on screen. One label map for statuses, shared by the badge, controls, chips and the applicant side.
2. **One number, one definition:** conversion and days to join are defined once, in KPI. Other screens link to KPI instead of computing their own version.
3. **One place per action:** a record's actions live on its detail page; lists are for finding records. Exports are list-page CSVs plus detail-page PDFs.
4. **Stat cards only when they add something:** never a count already shown by a segment, section or list on the same screen. A card that links somewhere must open a list showing the same number.
5. **Text:** headings and labels are short; no helper text that repeats a label; empty states are a title only; no line that is untrue.

     ```sql
     select c.slug, m.sheet_name, m.cell_ref, m.field_label, m.source_path
     from public.worksheet_mappings m
     join public.worksheet_customers c on c.id = m.customer_id
     where m.source_path ~* '(hair|eye|vaccin|hepatitis|typhoid|tetanus|measles|polio|salary|section13|section19|section10\.)'
     ```
